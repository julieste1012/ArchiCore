# Purpose

The purpose of this project is to design and develop a modern web-based Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) platform specialized in Document Management (DMS) for companies providing archival, custody, digitization and documentary administration services.

The first implementation target is MULTISERTEC LTDA.

---

# Business Problem

The current operation depends on spreadsheets, manual processes and dispersed information.

This generates:

- Slow document retrieval.
- Manual contract control.
- Limited traceability.
- Risk of human errors.
- Difficult reporting.
- Information duplication.

The ERP will centralize business information into a single platform.

---

# Business Objectives

The project aims to:

- Centralize all operational information.
- Digitize document management processes.
- Improve traceability.
- Reduce manual work.
- Facilitate contract administration.
- Support business growth.
- Comply with AGN regulations.

---

# Project Scope

The first version of the ERP will include:

- Customer management.
- Contract management.
- Warehouse management.
- Physical archive management.
- Documentary hierarchy.
- Barcode management.
- Documentary loans.
- Digitization requests.
- Billing support.
- User and role management.
- Audit logs.
- Dashboards.

---

# Out of Scope

The first version will not include:

- Payroll.
- Human Resources.
- Electronic invoicing.
- Accounting replacement.
- CRM.
- Mobile applications.

The ERP will integrate with external systems when necessary instead of replacing them.

# Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when:

- All business processes are digital.
- Information is traceable.
- Documentary searches are immediate.
- Contract information is centralized.
- Reports are generated automatically.
- The platform is stable and maintainable.

---

# Constraints

- Single developer.
- Modular architecture.
- PostgreSQL database.
- ASP.NET Core backend.
- React frontend.
- Docker deployment.
- Colombian archival regulations (AGN).

---

# Risks

- Changes in business requirements.
- Growth of documentary volume.
- Future integrations.
- Regulatory updates.

The architecture must minimize the impact of these risks.

---

# Project Principles

- Business first.
- Documentation first.
- Simplicity over unnecessary complexity.
- Security by design.
- Scalability by architecture.
- Maintainability over shortcuts.

---

# Deliverables

- Complete documentation.
- Source code.
- Database.
- REST API.
- Frontend.
- Deployment environment.
- User documentation.
