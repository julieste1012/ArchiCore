# Purpose

This document defines the business services offered by MULTISERTEC LTDA.

A business service represents a commercial capability provided to customers through one or more contracts.

Business processes described in later documents belong to one or more of these services.

---

# Service Portfolio

The initial version of the ERP supports the following services.

---

## Documentary Management

Description

Management, organization and custody of physical documentary archives according to AGN regulations.

Main activities

- Reception
- Classification
- Organization
- Labeling
- Physical storage
- Custody
- Retrieval
- Documentary loans
- Documentary return

---

## Documentary Custody

Description

Long-term preservation of documentary assets.

Main activities

- Storage
- Inventory
- Physical control
- Traceability

---

## Digitization

Description

Conversion of physical documentary assets into digital information.

Main activities

- Scan requests
- Image generation
- Quality verification
- Digital association

---

## Technical Information Management

Description

Administration of technical documentation for industrial clients.

Examples

- Engineering documentation
- Technical drawings
- Process documentation

---

## Reprography

Description

Printing and reproduction services.

Examples

- Printing
- Plotting
- Copying
- Finishing

---

## Equipment Rental

Description

Rental of office equipment.

Examples

- Printers
- Plotters
- Scanners
- Office equipment

---

## Information Processing

Description

Processing and organization of documentary information.

Examples

- Metadata capture
- Documentary indexing
- Documentary organization

---

## Engineering Support

Description

Engineering support services.

Examples

- Smart 3D
- Smart P&ID
- Smart Instrumentation
- Smart Review
- AutoCAD

---

# Service Characteristics

Every service may define:

- Billing rules
- Operational processes
- Required personnel
- Required equipment
- Required documentary resources
- Performance indicators

---

# Contract Relationship

A contract may include one or more services.

Each service may appear in multiple contracts.

The relationship is many-to-many.

---

# ERP Scope

Version 1 of the ERP focuses primarily on:

- Documentary Management
- Documentary Custody
- Digitization
- Billing Support
- Equipment Rental

The remaining services are incorporated progressively without changing the architecture.
