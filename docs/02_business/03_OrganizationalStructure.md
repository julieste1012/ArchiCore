# Purpose

This document defines the organizational structure of the business managed by the DMS ERP.

It describes the relationships between customers, contracts, projects, warehouses, documentary assets and personnel.

This document represents the business organization independently of the software implementation.

---

# Business Structure

The business is organized around service contracts.

Each contract generates operational activities that are executed by MULTISERTEC personnel.

The documentary assets always belong to the customer.

MULTISERTEC provides custody and management services.

# Customer

A customer is the contracting company.

Characteristics:

- May have multiple contracts.
- May have contracts in different cities.
- Owns its documentary assets.
- Receives documentary services from MULTISERTEC.

---

# Contract

Every contract belongs to exactly one customer.

Each contract defines:

- Contract period
- Contract value
- Contracted services
- Storage capacity
- Billing conditions

A customer may have multiple active contracts simultaneously.

---

# Project

Operational execution of a contract.

Each contract generates one operational project.

The project is managed by a Project Coordinator.

The project is responsible for daily documentary operations.

---

# Warehouse

Each project is assigned one or more warehouse spaces.

Warehouses store:

- Documentary material
- Equipment
- Other contractual assets

Warehouses are divided into physical locations.

# Personnel Assignment

Each project has assigned personnel.

Typical assignment:

- Project Coordinator
- Archive Technician
- Archive Assistants
- Digitization Operators

Personnel may participate in multiple projects if authorized.

---

# Documentary Ownership

Ownership never changes.

The documentary assets always remain the property of the customer.

MULTISERTEC acts as custodian.

---

# Equipment

Equipment may be assigned to contracts.

Examples:

- Printers
- Scanners
- Plotters
- Office equipment

Equipment assignment is independent from documentary storage.

---

# Contract Closure

When a contract ends:

- Documentary inventory is verified.
- Pending loans are resolved.
- Documentation is returned to the customer.
- Storage capacity is released.
- Contract is archived.

Historical information remains available.

---

# Organizational Principles

The organization follows these principles:

- Contracts organize operations.
- Projects execute contracts.
- Warehouses support projects.
- Documentary assets belong to customers.
- Personnel execute business processes.
- Every operation must be traceable.
